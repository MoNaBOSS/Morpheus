title: Morpheus Phase 5 cost and quality evidence
type: ai-coding-rule
appliesTo:
  - gateway-backend-communication
---

Model routing must preserve objective ownership, bounded context, persona and
execution authority. Failed or outcome-uncertain requests still consume attempt
allowance. Never retry without a bound or switch to another billing account on
exhaustion. Record correlation ids, validated units and outcomes, never prompts,
audio, transcripts, credential headers or arbitrary response bodies.

Report Core, OpenClaw, STT, TTS, validation and plugin/service coverage separately.
Transcript cost is runtime-reported evidence, not a reconciled provider bill.
Unknown costs and missing records cannot imply zero spend. Do not advertise a
global cap until all paid routes are mediated. Offline fixtures validate software
only; live quality and prices require explicit evidence and spending authority.
